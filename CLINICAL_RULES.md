\## General Data Validation Rules



1\. Patient\_ID must be unique.

2\. Age must be within the configured valid range.

3\. Department must match an approved department name.

4\. Diagnosis must match one of the diagnoses generated by the program.

5\. Length\_of\_Stay must be a positive whole number.

6\. Billing\_Amount must be a non-negative number.

7\. Admission\_Date must use the expected date format.

8\. Required fields must not be missing.



All records are synthetic and intended for educational and software-testing purposes only.
